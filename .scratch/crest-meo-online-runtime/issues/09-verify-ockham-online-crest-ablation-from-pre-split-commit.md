The specific motivation to test is:

Root faults in microservice systems can trigger latency inflation, retry/backpressure, log bursts, trace volume shifts, status/endpoint shifts, and downstream error propagation. The most visible anomaly is often on a victim service, not the root service. The key online-ranking problem is therefore not missing anomaly signals, but mixing root-local mutation evidence, propagated symptom evidence, and neutral local anomaly energy into one suspiciousness score.
